## Race Strategy Notes

### 10k (Jul 12)
- Warm-up: 10min easy jog + 4x100m strides + dynamic stretches
- First 2km: settle at 4:50/km (don't go out too fast)
- Km 3-7: lock in at 4:45/km
- Km 8-10: push to 4:35-4:40/km if feeling strong
- Goal: negative split, finish strong

### HM (Sep 20)
- Warm-up: 10min easy jog + 4x100m strides
- Km 1-5: 5:05/km (slightly conservative)
- Km 6-15: 5:00/km (cruise)
- Km 16-21: 4:50-4:55/km if legs allow (negative split)
- Fueling: gel at km 8 and km 15
- Hydrate every 5km

## Gym Programming Notes

### Leg Day A (Quad-dominant)
- Back squats (primary), RDLs, leg press, leg curl, calf raises
- Progressive overload: add 2.5kg/week on squats when hitting rep targets

### Leg Day B (Posterior/unilateral)
- Bulgarian split squats, hip thrusts, leg extensions, hamstring curls/Nordics, calf raises
- Focus on single-leg stability and posterior chain

### Push (when wrist allows)
- Bench press, OHP, incline DB press, lateral raises, tricep work
- Maintenance volume — not trying to progress while running is primary

### Pull (when wrist allows)
- Pull-ups, barbell/cable rows, face pulls, bicep curls
- Maintenance volume — preserve muscle, don't chase strength PRs

### Priority Order
1. Running sessions (never skip)
2. Leg days (never skip — muscle preservation)
3. Push/pull (do when recovered, skip if fatigued from running)

## Nutrition Reminders
- Caloric surplus of 200-300kcal on training days for lean bulk
- 1.8-2.0g protein/kg (125-140g/day)
- Carb-heavy pre-run meals (2-3hrs before)
- Post-run: protein + carbs within 30min
- Hydrate: 2.5-3L water daily minimum
