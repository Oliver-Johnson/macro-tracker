Getting Started

Install Macrolog

Macrolog is a Progressive Web App — add it to your home screen and it behaves just like a native app. Pick your browser below for step-by-step instructions.

1

Open the app in Safari

Visit macroslog.co.uk in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.

2

Tap the Share button

It's the box with an arrow pointing up, in the bottom toolbar.

3

Tap "Add to Home Screen"

Scroll down the share sheet until you find it.

4

Tap "Add"

Confirm with the Add button in the top right. Macrolog now lives on your home screen.

Safari — Add to Home Screen
1

Open the app in Chrome

Visit macroslog.co.uk in Chrome on Android or desktop.

2

Open the menu

Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top right.

3

Choose install

Tap "Add to Home Screen" on Android, or "Install Macrolog…" on desktop.

4

Confirm

Tap "Add" or "Install" to finish.

Chrome — Install
1

Open the app in Edge

Visit macroslog.co.uk in Microsoft Edge.

2

Open the menu

Tap the three-dot menu.

3

Choose install

Tap "Add to phone" or "Install site as app".

4

Confirm

Confirm to add Macrolog to your device.

Edge — Install site as app
1

Open the app in Samsung Internet

Visit macroslog.co.uk in Samsung Internet.

2

Open the menu

Tap the menu (three horizontal lines).

3

Add page to home screen

Tap "Add page to" → "Home screen".

Samsung — Add page to Home screen
!

Firefox doesn't support installing PWAs

Firefox on Android and iOS does not currently support installing Progressive Web Apps to the home screen.

→

Use Chrome or Safari instead

For the best experience, open macroslog.co.uk in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iOS) and follow those steps.

Firefox — not supported
