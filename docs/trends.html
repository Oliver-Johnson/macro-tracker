Chapter 03 — Reading the Charts
▸

Bars are daily values; the line is your rolling average. The line smooths out day-to-day noise so the trend is clear.

▸

The target line is a dashed reference. See at a glance which days landed above or below your goal.

▸

Tap a bar to see that day's exact values.

▸

The weight chart is separate. It appears once you've logged weight, with its own rolling average.
