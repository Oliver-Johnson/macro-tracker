Custom Foods & Quick Add

Log a food once and Macrolog remembers it. Build a personal library over time, or skip the search entirely and enter macros directly.

Chapter 01 — Saving Custom Foods
▸

When you log a food manually, it's automatically saved to My Foods — no extra step.

▸

Next time you search for it, it appears at the top of your results for quick re-logging.

▸

Edit or delete saved foods any time from the My Foods list in Settings.

Chapter 02 — Quick Add
▸

The Quick tab in Log Food lets you enter calories and macros directly — no food name needed.

▸

Useful for rough estimates or foods you only track by macros.

▸

Quick-add entries appear in your food log like any other entry.

Chapter 03 — Tips
▸

Build up your My Foods library over time for faster logging every day.

▸

Recipes you save also appear in search, making portion logging easy.
