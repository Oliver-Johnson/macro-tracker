Feature Guide

Barcode Scanner

Point your camera at a product barcode, or give Macrolog a photo of one, and it looks the product up instantly. Results are cached, so foods you scan often appear in a flash.

01 — How Scanning Works

How scanning works.

Macrolog reads barcodes live through your camera and matches them against a huge open product database — no typing required.

  • Uses your camera to detect barcodes in real time.
  • Looks the barcode up in the Open Food Facts database.
  • Results are cached — no repeated lookups for the same product.
02 — No Camera?

No camera? Use a photo.

Most laptops and desktops don’t have a camera you can point at a packet. Take a photo with your phone, or screenshot a barcode from a shopping site, and Macrolog reads the barcode from the image instead.

  • On the Scan tab, tap Barcode from Image and pick the photo.
  • On a computer, drag the photo onto the Log Food page, or copy a screenshot and paste it (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac).
  • Recipes work the same way: in an ingredient’s Scan dialog, use Barcode from Image or drop or paste the photo.
  • Sideways, upside-down and tilted barcodes are fine. A full-size phone photo works even when the barcode is small in it; a blurry one may not.
  • If a photo won’t read, type the number under the barcode into Search. The product comes up first, marked Barcode.
03 — Scanning Tips
Pro Tip

Scanning tips.

A clean, steady read is everything. If a code won’t catch, these adjustments almost always fix it.

  • Hold steady — the scanner needs a sharp, well-lit barcode.
  • Move closer or further if it doesn’t detect — 15–25cm is usually ideal.
  • If the barcode won’t scan, try “Barcode from Image”, “Upload Label” or “Paste Text” instead.
04 — Product Not Found?

Product not found?

Not every product is in the database yet — but a barcode you add once is yours forever, and instant on every future scan.

  • If a barcode isn’t in the database, you’ll be prompted to enter details manually.
  • Your entry is saved to My Foods — next time you scan it, it appears instantly.
  • Alternatively, scan or photograph the nutrition label directly.
