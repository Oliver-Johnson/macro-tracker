Feature Guide

Nutrition Label Parser

Turn any nutrition label into a logged entry in seconds. Scan it, upload a photo, or paste the text — Macrolog reads off the values for you.

01 — Three Ways to Parse a Label

Three ways in.

Whatever you have to hand — a physical label, a photo in your gallery, or copied text — there is a fast path to a logged entry.

  • 📷 Scan with camera — point at the label and tap “Scan Label”.
  • 🖼️ Upload a photo — tap “Upload Label” and choose an image from your gallery.
  • 📋 Paste text — copy the nutrition text, paste it into the text box, and tap Parse.
02 — What Gets Extracted

What gets extracted.

The core macros come through every time. When the label lists more detail, Macrolog captures that too.

  • Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fat — always extracted.
  • Fibre, Sugar, Saturated Fat, Sodium — captured when available on the label.
  • Always review the extracted values before logging — confirm they look right first.
03 — Tips for Best Results
Pro Tip

Tips for best results.

Parsing is only as good as what it can see. A few small habits make extraction far more accurate.

  • Use good lighting, keep the label flat, and avoid glare.
  • Keep the full label in frame — include the “per serving” and “per 100g” columns.
  • For text paste, include the full nutrition facts table, not just a few selected values.
